Beyond the Hype: The 2026 Shift to Agentic Health AI

In previous years, we marveled at Large Language Models (LLMs) that could summarize a chart. In 2026, the paradigm has shifted toward Agentic AI—autonomous systems that don't just "chat," but "act." These agents navigate the EHR, coordinate between departments, and proactively flag clinical deteriorations before the first symptom appears.
